## Permanent application design rules

- All five surfaces (Music, Movies & TV, Read, Vault, Settings), including their categories and navigation: no decorative separator lines, whether dotted or solid, horizontal or vertical. Use subtly contrasting theme-owned **data-row** backgrounds and spacing, following the user's track-table reference. The latest Windows review explicitly removes broad grey backgrounds behind artwork shelves and section headings. A sidebar's tonal surface continues to the window's top edge while its controls stay below the drag strip. Keep control outlines, enclosing panel frames, focus rings, progress/seek indicators and authored player visuals; these are not content separators. Active/hover/focus states must remain distinguishable and keyboard accessible.
- Use shared Tsugi palette and font tokens; avoid fixed blue/purple backgrounds that clash with other themes. Panel colors must be derived consistently from the selected palette.
- Keep the TSUGI brand and actual Read, Books, Music and Vault section labels. YOMI/OTO/KURA are reference-pack placeholders only. Use established Brush/Tsugi vector icons; do not copy prototype logos or icon images into navigation or controls.
- Preserve existing controls and navigation. Use the real content/artwork, never invented data or fake controls.
- No new package on every small change; validate each step and package a coherent, tested milestone.
